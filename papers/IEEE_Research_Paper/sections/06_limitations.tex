\section{Limitations, Threats to Validity \& Ethical Considerations}
\label{sec:limitations}
\noindent\textbf{1) Observability \& Banking Silos:} Fiat banking operates under statutory secrecy (RFPA, GDPR), restricting institutions to local 1-hop visibility and motivating federated graph architectures ($15.8\%$--$44.5\%$ F1 on IBM-AMLSim).\par\vspace{0.2ex}
\noindent\textbf{2) Ground-Truth Boundaries:} High scores on crypto benchmarks reflect law enforcement seizures forming dense homophilic clusters ($h > 0.92$), validating algorithmic robustness rather than unconstrained laundering evasion (e.g., Hawala or TBML).\par\vspace{0.2ex}
\noindent\textbf{3) Label Latency \& Calibration:} Illicit labels incur confirmation delays ($\Delta T_{\text{delay}} \in [30, 90]$\,days). Assembling calibration buffers ($n_1 \ge 100$) requires pooling historical alerts; conformal bounds do not confer statutory clearance.\par\vspace{0.2ex}
\noindent\textbf{4) Cold-Start Invariants:} On cold-start entities ($\deg(u) \le 2$), C-STGB attenuates graph weights ($\alpha_u \to 0.08$) and transfers mass to flow invariants ($\mathbf{z}_{\text{inv}}$), securing $89.60\%$ F1 via tabular velocity rather than graph topology.\par\vspace{0.2ex}
\noindent\textbf{5) Construct Overlap \& Fairness:} Edge-trust supervision uses hub thresholds ($\deg_{\text{out}} \ge 50$) on $\mathcal{E}_{\text{train}}$, sharing inductive bias with degree-matched camouflage; generalization is verified under gradient attacks. Demographic fairness warrants audits under ECOA.\par\vspace{0.2ex}
\noindent\textbf{6) Model Governance:} LLM-assisted SAR drafting is strictly an auxiliary prototype; autonomous filings are prohibited under BSA regulations and Federal Reserve SR 11-7 model risk management guidance~\cite{fed2011sr117}. Evaluated data comprise de-identified public ledgers or synthetic simulations with zero PII.\par
