   \vspace*{0.3cm}
   {\Large\textbf{ABSTRACT}}\\[0.5cm]
\end{center}

Financial money laundering accounts for an estimated \$800 billion to \$2 trillion annually (2\% to 5\% of global GDP), posing severe threats to international economic stability and statutory compliance. Although Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) have emerged as the premier machine learning paradigm for transaction graphs, production deployment faces three technical bottlenecks: (1)~\textit{continuous-time velocity evasion}, where syndicates operate across microsecond bursts and 90-day dormant holding chains; (2)~\textit{adversarial topological camouflage}, where fraudsters route through high-degree merchant hubs to induce feature over-smoothing; and (3)~\textit{extreme base-rate class imbalance} ($<0.05\%$ illicit incidence), causing standard decision boundaries ($\tau = 0.50$) to trigger severe recall collapse.

To overcome these challenges, this thesis presents \textbf{C-STGB} (\underline{C}onformal \underline{S}patio-\underline{T}emporal \underline{G}raph\underline{B}oost), an end-to-end framework integrating: (i)~a continuous Tri-Band Temporal Attention Bank capturing multi-scale dynamics; (ii)~a learnable MLP Edge-Gating filter that suppresses 65.9\% of adversarial camouflage noise; (iii)~Typology-Clustered Latent GraphSMOTE with localized link synthesis raising standalone GNN recall on Elliptic-v1 from 10.33\% to 90.10\%; (iv)~domain-informed physical mass-flow conservation and 12-dimensional canonical invariants; (v)~dynamic Bayes decision thresholding; and (vi)~class-conditional Conformal Risk Control (CRC) delivering distribution-free finite-sample coverage guarantees while automating $>99.4\%$ of volume through straight-through execution tiers. Extensive empirical evaluations across 14 financial transaction networks totaling 9,534,980 entities and 9,528,355 transactions demonstrate that C-STGB achieves top performance across relational cryptocurrency and banking streams ($91.42\%$ F1 on Elliptic-v1, $99.10\%$ on Elliptic-v2, and $68.05\%$ macro F1 globally), while guaranteeing $0.45$\,ms Fast-Path inference latency satisfying real-time core-banking SLAs.
