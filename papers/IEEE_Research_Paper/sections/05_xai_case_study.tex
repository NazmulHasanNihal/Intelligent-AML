\subsection{15-Node Smurfing Fan-In \& Peeling Chain Forensics}

    \item \textbf{Layering \& Wash-Cycle Tier ($M \to L_{1\dots 5}$):} Mule accounts rapidly funnel assets to five intermediary layering nodes ($L_1$--$L_5$). These nodes exhibit near-unity flow conservation ratios ($\Phi_{\text{flow}} \approx 0.994$) and form cyclic wash loops ($L_1 \to L_2 \to L_3 \to L_1$) designed to obfuscate transaction provenance through recursive volume churn.
    \item \textbf{Integration \& Cash-Out Tier ($L \to C_{1\dots 4}$):} Cleaned funds are consolidated across four high-velocity exit accounts ($C_1$--$C_4$) linked to off-ramp cryptocurrency exchanges.
    \item \textbf{Camouflage Filtration:} Three spurious edges attached to high-volume commercial merchants (e.g., $M_1 \to M_5$ and $L_4 \to M_3$) are assigned near-zero gating weights ($\mathbf{g}_{ij} \le 0.042$), preventing message passing over-smoothing.
\end{enumerate}
