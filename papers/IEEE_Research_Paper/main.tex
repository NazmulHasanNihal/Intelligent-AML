\begin{abstract}
Financial money laundering accounts for an estimated \$800 billion to \$2 trillion annually (2\% to 5\% of global GDP), posing severe threats to international financial stability. Although Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) capture relational transaction structures, their deployment in Anti-Money Laundering (AML) surveillance is hindered by five foundational challenges: (1) long-dwell hibernation evasion; (2) adversarial topological camouflage through high-degree commercial hubs; (3) extreme class imbalance under an illicit-event prevalence below 0.05\% inducing recall collapse; (4) cold-start graph isolation; and (5) uncalibrated decision uncertainty. To address these challenges jointly, we propose \textbf{C-STGB} (\underline{C}onformal \underline{S}patio-\underline{T}emporal \underline{G}raph\underline{B}oost), a unified geometric deep learning framework integrating continuous tri-band harmonic temporal attention, learnable context-aware edge-trust gating, typology-clustered latent GraphSMOTE, evidence-adaptive graph-tabular fusion, cost-sensitive Bayes risk optimization, and Class-Conditional Conformal Risk Control (CRC). Evaluated across 13 diverse financial benchmarks comprising over 8.81 million entities and 11.42 million transactions over five random seeds, C-STGB achieves the highest performance among evaluated baselines ($91.42 \pm 0.65\%$ F1 on Elliptic-v1, $86.54 \pm 0.82\%$ F1 on Elliptic-v2, and $94.62 \pm 0.55\%$ F1 on Ethereum Phishing), reduces manual compliance review volume by $>99.4\%$, and delivers a streaming single-event latency of $0.45$\,ms (P99: $0.82$\,ms under Fast-Path mode; $2.10$\,ms under full multi-scale GNN mode), satisfying real-time payment authorization SLAs ($31,200\text{ tx/s}$).
\end{abstract}
