\noindent
\textbf{Scope and Alignment with ESWA Mandate:}\\
In direct alignment with the core mandate of \textit{Expert Systems with Applications}, this work develops and validates an end-to-end intelligent decision-support and surveillance expert system---named \textbf{C-STGB} (\underline{C}onformal \underline{S}patio-\underline{T}emporal \underline{G}raph\underline{B}oost)---specifically engineered for the critical domain of financial forensics and anti-money laundering (AML). We confirm that this work contains \textbf{no military or defense applications}. Rather than presenting an abstract machine learning heuristic or nature-inspired metaphor, C-STGB tackles five foundational domain hurdles in banking compliance: continuous-time velocity evasion, adversarial topological camouflage, extreme class imbalance ($\pi < 0.05\%$), cold-start account sparsity, and uncalibrated decision uncertainty.

\vspace{0.6em}
\noindent
\textbf{Key Contributions and Substantive Findings:}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=2pt,topsep=2pt]
    \item \textbf{Continuous-Time Multi-Scale Temporal Graph Architecture:} C-STGB unifies continuous harmonic temporal embeddings, Hawkes arrival intensity, and a context-aware edge-trust filter ($\hat{g}_{ij}$) that prunes 65.9\% of camouflage chaff connecting to high-degree banking hubs.
    \item \textbf{Flow-Conserving Typology Oversampling:} A latent GraphSMOTE mechanism clustered by laundering archetypes with Asymmetric Focal Tversky optimization, formally proven to preserve point-level mass-flow conservation invariants.
    \item \textbf{Finite-Sample Risk Guarantees under Drift:} Class-Conditional Conformal Risk Control establishing $\ge 99.0\%$ empirical true-label coverage under within-class exchangeability, non-asymptotic drift bounds, and online Martingale tracking via Adaptive Conformal Inference (ACI). Decisive singletons route $>99.4\%$ of volume through automated straight-through tiers, reducing officer review queues to $0.50\%$.
    \item \textbf{Large-Scale Empirical Corpus \& System Latency:} Evaluated across 14 financial transaction networks ($9.53\text{M}$ entities, $9.53\text{M}$ transactions across 182 evaluation pairs over five locked seeds), C-STGB achieves $67.26\%$ unweighted macro F1, delivering $+43.49\text{ pp}$ mean uplift over deep spatial GNNs ($W=105.0, p_{\text{adj}} < 0.001$). Streaming Fast-Path inference executes in $0.45$\,ms per single event ($31,200\text{ tx/s}$).
    \item \textbf{Compliance Decision Support Prototype:} An auxiliary module assisting examiners in compiling verifiable FinCEN Form 111 XML dossiers under Federal Reserve SR 11-7 model risk governance standards.
\end{itemize}
