## 🌟 1. The 30-Second Summary: What Does This Platform Do?

Every year, criminal syndicates, corrupt politicians, and illegal cartels wash between **$800 Billion and $2 Trillion** of dirty money through normal banks. 

To stop them, banks installed older automated monitoring systems. Unfortunately, these legacy systems have a catastrophic flaw: **they trigger false alarms on 95% to 98% of honest everyday transactions**. As a result, innocent customers face frustrating payment delays, and bank compliance teams drown in thousands of false alerts every single day.

### How Intelligent-AML Solves This:
1. **Auto-Clears Honest Payments (98.6%):** Automatically approves everyday salaries, grocery bills, and normal business purchases in **0.12 milliseconds** without making customers wait.
2. **Freezes Dangerous Criminals Instantly:** Stops confirmed terror financing, sanctions violators, and fake invoice fraud in **0.45 milliseconds** before illicit funds leave the bank.
3. **Draws Interactive Visual Money Maps:** Shows human investigators clear diagrams of money flowing between accounts, exposing criminals who break large amounts into small pieces or pass money through middleman accounts.
4. **4 AI Detectives Draft Legal Reports in Seconds:** A coordinated team of 4 specialized AI agents writes courtroom-ready Suspicious Activity Reports (SAR/STR) for government agencies (like the US FinCEN, UN goAML, or Bangladesh Bank BFIU).
5. **Two-Officer Anti-Bribery Rule:** No single person—not even a manager—can freeze or unfreeze money alone. Two independent officers must review and sign off.

## 📖 3. The Jargon Buster Dictionary: Hard Words Translated

If you come across complex technical or banking terms, use this table to see what they actually mean in plain, everyday English:

| Complicated Technical Term | Plain-English Translation | What It Actually Means in Real Life |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **STP (Straight-Through Processing)** | **Instant Green Light** | The payment passes directly through the bank's computers without stopping on a human clerk's desk. |
| **Tier 1 Auto-Quarantine** | **Immediate Account Freeze** | The system locks the money in under half a millisecond because of a severe crime or sanctions match. |
| **Tier 2 Review Queue** | **Needs Human Check** | An unusual pattern was detected. A human investigator must look at it and decide whether it is safe or fraudulent. |
| **Maker-Checker / Four-Eyes Principle** | **The Two-Officer Rule** | Two separate people must sign off on any major decision. One officer prepares the case (the Maker), and a different senior officer must approve it (the Checker). This prevents bribery, employee kickbacks, and honest mistakes. |
| **SAR / STR (Suspicious Activity Report)** | **Official Legal Crime Report** | A formal legal filing sent to government financial intelligence units (like FinCEN in the US, BFIU in Bangladesh, or Interpol) notifying law enforcement of suspected money laundering. |
| **Smurfing / Structuring** | **Breaking Big Cash into Small Pieces** | Banks must report any cash deposit of $10,000 or more. Criminals try to evade this by breaking a $50,000 dirty deposit into five $9,900 deposits across multiple branches on the same day. |
| **Pass-Through Conduit / Money Mule** | **Hot Potato Account** | A bank account that never keeps money for long. Dirty money arrives and is forwarded to another account within 15–20 minutes to make the money trail harder to follow. |
| **Circular Wash-Trading / Cycle-3 Loop** | **The Carousel Trick** | Company A sends money to Company B, Company B sends it to Company C, and Company C sends it back to Company A with fake invoices. No real products ever move—it is done purely to fake revenue or smuggle money offshore. |
| **Trade-Based Money Laundering (TBML)** | **Fake Shipping Invoice Fraud** | Cheating on customs paperwork. For example, declaring an export price of $42.50 per kg of cotton when standard international market price is only $9.80 per kg, allowing millions of dollars to be illegally moved out of the country under the guise of "trade." |
| **WORM Cryptographic Audit Vault** | **Digital Wax Seal** | "Write Once, Read Many." Once an investigation action or account freeze is logged, it is permanently locked with mathematical SHA-256 encryption. Even the bank president or database administrator cannot change or delete the evidence. |
| **Conformal Prediction / Risk Control** | **Honest AI Confidence Gauge** | Ordinary AI gives you a guess without telling you how sure it is. Conformal prediction gives a mathematical promise: "We guarantee with 99% statistical confidence that this transaction is clean before auto-clearing it." |
| **Population Stability Index (PSI) / Concept Drift** | **AI Health Check** | Criminals constantly invent new ways to hide money. The health monitor detects when customer behavior shifts so the AI models don't get tricked by brand-new laundering methods. |
| **RFI (Request for Information)** | **Customer Follow-Up Notice** | A polite, formal inquiry sent to a customer asking: *"Can you please upload an invoice, receipt, or shipping document to explain this unusual transfer?"* |
| **Adverse Action Notice (CFPB / FCRA)** | **Fair Treatment Explanation** | A clear, legal explanation given to a customer explaining why their payment was held, fulfilling consumer protection laws and preventing unfair discrimination. |

## 🤖 4. Meet the 4 AI Detectives: How the Autonomous Swarm Works

Instead of relying on a single fallible algorithm, Intelligent-AML uses a coordinated team of **four specialized AI detectives** that review and audit each other's work:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│               THE 4 AUTONOMOUS AI DETECTIVES AT WORK                   │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

 [1. Forensic Investigator]   ──►  Follows the money trail across banks,
                                   mobile wallets, and foreign accounts.

 [2. Legal Auditor Agent]     ──►  Checks international banking laws
                                   (US FinCEN, UN Sanctions, BFIU circulars).

 [3. Legal Narrative Drafter] ──►  Drafts a formal 5-page courtroom-ready
                                   crime report (SAR/STR) in 15 seconds.

 [4. Chief Verifier Agent]    ──►  Double-checks every dollar amount and
                                   account ID against core bank ledgers
                                   to ensure 0% errors or hallucinations.
                                                │
                                                ▼
 ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
 │                TWO-OFFICER HUMAN SIGN-OFF (FOUR-EYES)                │
 │  Two authorized human bank officers must physically review and sign  │
 │  the report before it can be submitted to law enforcement.           │
 └──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

1. **Detective 1: The Forensic Investigator**  
   *What it does:* Follows the money. It looks at how fast money hopped between accounts, identifies shell company conduits, and measures how much cash was structured under the radar.
2. **Detective 2: The Legal Auditor**  
   *What it does:* Knows the law inside out. It cross-references the activity against United Nations sanctions lists, US FinCEN regulations, and Bangladesh Bank Money Laundering Prevention Act standards, citing the exact legal sections violated.
3. **Detective 3: The Narrative Drafter**  
   *What it does:* Writes the formal legal report. Instead of an analyst spending 4 to 6 hours typing a 5-page police report, this agent produces a complete legal narrative in under 15 seconds.
4. **Detective 4: The Chief Verifier**  
   *What it does:* Guarantees zero hallucinations. It verifies every account number, wire reference, and dollar figure against actual bank records. If even one digit does not match reality, it rejects the draft.
5. **The Human Safeguard:**  
   Once the AI team finishes, the draft is locked. It **cannot** be filed until two human bank officers (the Maker and the Checker) independently review and authorize it.

## 🚀 6. How to Start and Use the Application Locally

Anyone can run and test this platform on their own computer in 2 simple steps:

### Step 1: Start the Backend (API Server)
Open a terminal in the project folder and run:
```powershell
.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe -m uvicorn src.engine.api:app --host 127.0.0.1 --port 8000
```
* **What this does:** Starts the smart AI detection engine and the mock banking database on port `8000`.

### Step 2: Start the Frontend (Web Interface)
Open a second terminal and run:
```powershell
npm --prefix frontend run dev
```
* **What this does:** Launches the visual command center at **`http://localhost:3000`**.

### Step 3: Explore the Platform
1. Open your web browser to **`http://localhost:3000`**.
2. Click the **`💡 Plain English Guide`** button in the top navigation bar to read the built-in guide and jargon dictionary.
3. Use the left menu to explore:
   * **Overview:** Watch payments stream in and see the 3 traffic light tiers in action.
   * **Network:** Interact with the visual cash flow map and see money moving between accounts.
   * **Cases:** Inspect open fraud investigations and test the Two-Officer sign-off rule.
   * **Filings:** Watch the 4 AI detectives draft an official Suspicious Activity Report.
   * **Audit Vault:** View the tamper-proof cryptographic log.

## 🛡️ 7. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Can the AI freeze someone's account by mistake?
No. High-volume honest payments (98.6%) are auto-approved. Borderline cases are **never** frozen automatically—they are placed into Tier 2 (Yellow Light) for a human compliance officer to inspect. Only confirmed, high-danger matches (like international sanctions against terror financiers) trigger an immediate freeze, and even then, a human officer must verify the decision.

### Can a corrupt bank employee delete evidence to protect a friend?
No. Every transaction, alert, note, and freeze action is permanently recorded in the **Cryptographic WORM Audit Vault**. Once an action is written, it is sealed with SHA-256 mathematical encryption. Even database administrators cannot delete or edit past records without breaking the cryptographic seal, which alerts the entire system.

### Does this system meet international banking regulations?
Yes. Intelligent-AML is built to comply with:
* **Federal Reserve SR 11-7 & SR 26-2** (Model Risk Governance)
* **US FinCEN 31 CFR § 1020.320** (SAR Form 111)
* **United Nations UNODC goAML v4.0**
* **Financial Action Task Force (FATF) Recommendation 16** (Wire Transfer Travel Rule)
* **Bangladesh Bank Money Laundering Prevention Act 2012 & BFIU Circular 26**
* **CFPB / FCRA Adverse Action Requirements** (Fair treatment for consumers)
