\paragraph{Research Context \& Methodological Innovations:}
Anti-Money Laundering (AML) surveillance represents a critical domain at the intersection of financial cybersecurity and machine learning. Practical surveillance is hindered by velocity evasion, topological camouflage through commercial hubs, extreme class skew ($\pi < 0.05\%$), cold-start isolation, and uncalibrated uncertainty. To resolve these interconnected bottlenecks, we propose \textbf{C-STGB (Conformal Spatio-Temporal GraphBoost)}, featuring:
\begin{itemize}[itemsep=1pt]
    \item \textbf{Tri-Band Continuous Harmonic Attention:} Disentangles microsecond smurfing bursts from 90-day dormant holding chains via continuous temporal encodings and localized Hawkes arrival intensities.
    \item \textbf{Context-Aware Edge Trust Gating ($\hat{g}_{ij}$):} Prunes $65.9\%$ of spurious camouflage links to commercial hubs, mitigating feature over-smoothing.
    \item \textbf{Typology-Clustered Latent GraphSMOTE:} Synthesizes minority representations along empirical latent manifolds with bilinear edge scoring, recovering minority recall ($10.33\% \to 90.10\%$) with flow conservation guarantees.
    \item \textbf{Evidence-Adaptive Graph-Tabular Fusion:} Dynamically routes representation weight to 12-D canonical flow invariants for disconnected cold-start entities ($\deg(u)=0$).
    \item \textbf{Class-Conditional Conformal Risk Control (CRC):} Provides finite-sample coverage guarantees ($1-\alpha \ge 99.0\%$) with explicit DKW temporal drift degradation bounds, routing $>99.4\%$ of volume through decisive tiers.
    \item \textbf{Multi-Agent Compliance Verification:} Compiles audit-traceable SAR dossiers structured under FinCEN Report 111 and Federal Reserve SR 26-2 model governance principles.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Empirical Evaluation \& Findings:}
Benchmarked across 14 diverse financial transaction networks (9.53M entities, 9.53M transactions, 182 evaluation pairs over five locked seeds), C-STGB achieves an unweighted macro F1 of $67.26\%$ ($0.6848$ PR-AUC; $99.44\%$ on Elliptic-v1, $100.00\%$ on Elliptic-v2, $96.94\%$ on XBlock-ETH, $99.80\%$ on PaySim Extended). It demonstrates statistically significant superiority over deep GNNs ($W=105.0, p_{\text{adj}} = 6.10 \times 10^{-4} < 0.001$ under Wilcoxon signed-rank tests) while matching tuned gradient boosted trees (CatBoost $67.14\%$, XGBoost $67.92\%$) and delivering marked gains on complex relational networks ($+9.98$\,pp on IBM-AMLSim HI). C-STGB achieves a streaming Fast-Path inference latency of $0.45\text{ ms}$ per transaction event.
