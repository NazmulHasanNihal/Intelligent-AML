Real-World Scenario Catching a Circular Laundering Ring
1

The Crime (Smurfing)

A cartel splits $50,000 into 10 tiny transfers ($5k each) from Account A to Account B, passes it to C, and then back to A.

Traditional System Failure

Legacy rules only look at isolated rows. Since no single transfer is over the $10k reporting limit, the system sees nothing wrong.

2

The AI Detection (Layer 2)

The Intelligent AML engine builds a graph. It instantly sees the hidden geometry: a closed A → B → C → A loop.

The Mathematical Catch

The Burst-Aware math flags the unnatural speed of the transfers, and the AI flags the entire laundering ring with 98.5% confidence.

3

The Agentic Hand-off (Layer 4 & 5)

Instead of a confusing black-box alert, the AI generates a visual map of the cycle.

Human Action

The LLM Agent types out a full 10-page legal report. The Compliance Officer just reviews the AI's evidence and clicks Submit to the authorities.
