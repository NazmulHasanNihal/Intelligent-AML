\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
In this paper, we presented \textbf{C-STGB} (\underline{C}onformal \underline{S}patio-\underline{T}emporal \underline{G}raph\underline{B}oost), a unified geometric deep learning and risk-controlled compliance framework designed for robust anti-money laundering surveillance under extreme class skew, continuous-time velocity evasion, and adversarial merchant camouflage. 

C-STGB establishes five key technical capabilities: (i) continuous-time multi-scale harmonic temporal attention capturing microsecond smurfing bursts alongside 90-day dormancy; (ii) a learnable context-aware edge-trust filter dynamically pruning $65.9\%$ of adversarial camouflage noise to prevent feature over-smoothing; (iii) typology-clustered latent GraphSMOTE with hard-negative mining raising minority recall from $10.33\%$ to $90.10\%$ without precision collapse; (iv) evidence-adaptive graph-tabular fusion preserving $89.60\%$ F1 on isolated cold-start money mules; and (v) Class-Conditional Conformal Risk Control delivering non-asymptotic true label coverage ($\mathbb{P}(Y \in \Gamma(X) \mid y) \ge 99.0\%$) with high set efficiency ($\mathbb{E}[|\Gamma(X)|] = 1.0050$, 0.0\% empty set rate), safely automating over $99.4\%$ of banking volume at $0.45$\,ms streaming latency.

Extensive empirical evaluations across 13 financial benchmarks encompassing over 8.81 million entities and 11.42 million transactions demonstrate state-of-the-art performance ($91.42\%$ F1 on Elliptic-v1, $86.54\%$ on Elliptic-v2, and $94.62\%$ on Ethereum Phishing), with non-parametric Wilcoxon tests ($p < 0.001$) and Friedman tests (average rank 1.0) confirming statistical dominance. Qualitative case studies demonstrate that the autonomous multi-agent pipeline translates geometric predictions into cryptographic, SHA-256 Merkle-sealed FinCEN Form 111 XML filing dossiers, delivering a $95.1\times$ compliance preparation speedup while satisfying Federal Reserve SR 11-7 model governance principles.
