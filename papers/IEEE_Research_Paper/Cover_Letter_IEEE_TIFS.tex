\paragraph{Research Context \& Problem Formulation:}
Anti-Money Laundering (AML) surveillance represents a critical application of graph learning in financial forensics. While Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) capture relational transaction topologies, real-world deployment faces five foundational challenges: (1) continuous-time velocity evasion across micro-bursts and multi-month dormant periods; (2) adversarial topological camouflage via high-degree commercial hubs; (3) extreme class imbalance (illicit prevalence below 0.05\%) causing severe recall collapse; (4) cold-start graph isolation for disconnected entities; and (5) uncalibrated decision uncertainty.

\paragraph{Methodological Innovations:}
To resolve these interconnected bottlenecks, we introduce \textbf{C-STGB (Conformal Spatio-Temporal GraphBoost)}, a unified geometric framework featuring:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Tri-Band Continuous Harmonic Attention:} Disentangles microsecond smurfing bursts from 90-day dormant holding chains via continuous temporal encodings and localized Hawkes arrival intensities.
    \item \textbf{Context-Aware Edge Trust Gating ($\hat{g}_{ij}$):} Suppresses spurious camouflage linkages to high-degree commercial hubs, mitigating feature over-smoothing.
    \item \textbf{Typology-Clustered Latent GraphSMOTE:} Synthesizes virtual minority entities along latent manifolds with bilinear edge generation, recovering minority recall without precision collapse.
    \item \textbf{Evidence-Adaptive Graph-Tabular Fusion:} Dynamically transfers representation weight to 12-D canonical flow invariants for cold-start entities ($\deg(u)=0$).
    \item \textbf{Class-Conditional Conformal Risk Control (CRC):} Provides finite-sample coverage guarantees ($1-\alpha \ge 99.0\%$) under stated within-class exchangeability assumptions and routes over 99.4\% of volume through automated clearing/escalation tiers.
    \item \textbf{Multi-Agent Compliance Verification:} Generates audit-traceable FinCEN Form 111 XML dossiers with formal schema validation preventing unsupported entity references.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Empirical Evaluation \& Findings:}
Evaluated across 13 diverse financial transaction networks encompassing over 8.81 million entities and 11.42 million transactions across five random seeds, our proposed C-STGB achieves the highest performance among evaluated baselines across the benchmarks ($91.42 \pm 0.65\%$ F1 on Elliptic-v1, $86.54 \pm 0.82\%$ F1 on Elliptic-v2, and $94.62 \pm 0.55\%$ F1 on Ethereum Phishing). In our streaming inference evaluation, C-STGB achieves a single-event latency of $0.45\text{ ms}$ (Fast-Path) and $2.10\text{ ms}$ (Full GNN), well below the 10\,ms target latency.
